## Scope

In this section you should answer the following questions:

* What is the purpose of your database? The purpose is to build a database about artists and their songs in order to review statistics pertaining to the artists themselves and special
    characteristics about each song.
* Which people, places, things, etc. are you including in the scope of your database? I am including Artists Albulms, Songs, and flags such as if the song is a billboard hit or not.
* Which people, places, things, etc. are *outside* the scope of your database? Artists without any albulms or songs would not be included.

## Functional Requirements

In this section you should answer the following questions:

* What should a user be able to do with your database? A user should be able to query the database by artist, song, and characteristics.
